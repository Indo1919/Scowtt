# Scowtt case study

**Live site:** https://indo1919.github.io/Scowtt/

Scowtt is an AI startup that finds a company's best leads and books the meeting. I joined as a design intern, became Head of Design, and stayed on as a contractor through its $12M Series A. This case study walks through the work I did there: the first logo directions, the color palette, the original website and the product.

## What's inside

- **The problem:** explaining a product that works where nobody can see it.
- **The approach:** a "send a lead through it" explainer built around the product's two steps.
- **The brand:** my four logo directions, my color palette with contrast checks, and the message ladder.
- **The original website:** a working rebuild of the site on desktop and phone, with notes on each section.
- **The product:** my wireframes next to a rebuilt version, plus a working demo of the web and phone app with a guided tour.

## Notes

The whole case study is a single file, `index.html`, with no build step.

Scowtt's current logo and website were made later by a design team and aren't shown here. Everything inside the demo workspace, including people, companies and numbers, is fictional.
